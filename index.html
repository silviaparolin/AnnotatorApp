Codebook V2 Quick Guide (195 Images)

1. Blind Annotation

Evaluation is strictly blind. Image IDs, prompts, and ideological conditions are hidden. Judge only what is directly visible in the image.

2. "Not Applicable (N/A)" Option

Use Not Applicable / N/A whenever an attribute cannot be observed (e.g., facial expressions when people are seen from behind, or racial/ethnic diversity when only one person is shown).

3. No People & Conditional Questions

Selecting No people for V1 or V2 automatically sets dependent demographic/facial metrics (V3, V5, V6) to N/A. For V13, answering No automatically skips V14 and records no political/value text.

4. Scenario-Specific Metrics

Each scenario automatically loads its tailored metrics (speaker traits, crowd density, setting style, police framing, etc.). In the single-subject portrait scenario, V1–V2 are auto-filled from the main speaker's age and gender to avoid duplicate questions.
